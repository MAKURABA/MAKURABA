Making your own pages

Quick start
  1. Run python3 hub.py serve and open http://localhost:8000/edit.
  2. In Structure, tap ＋ beside a menu item and choose Group or Page.
  3. Open the page and add blocks: text, pictures, quotes and more.
  4. Link pages together with [[page-name]].
  5. When you like it, run python3 hub.py publish "my first page".

Clearing these demos

Everything on these pages is sample content. To start fresh, with only the five menu items, run:

python3 hub.py reset --yes

Nothing is deleted. Pages, pictures and examples move into a backup- folder.
