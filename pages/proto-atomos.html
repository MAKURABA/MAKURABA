PROTO-ATOMOS

# PROTO-ATOMOS I

The Primitive Unit of Distinction and the Geometric Assembly of Quality

MAKURABA · Revised edition

Abstract

This work defines a single discrete representational primitive, the proto-atomos, and builds from it a general system for describing and relating any distinguishable subject. A proto-atomos is the bounded line of distinction between the infinitesimal and the maximal expression of one quality, together with a determinate position on that line.

Part I sets out the unit: its limiting conditions, axioms, measurement standard, composition rules, layering, and worked examples. Part II joins the unit to the wider framework: Zero (the homogeneous reference condition), Delta (the relational distinction), Mono-Maximum (the closure of attainable configurations), and the χ-Reentrant Closure Calculus. It then specifies comparison, change over time, the observer, and a self-establishing descriptor network built on the primitive.

The central claim is that one primitive, applied recursively, is enough to relate every describable thing in a single, scale-independent geometry of quality.

# PART I — THE UNIT

1. Conventions and Notation

  • q — a quality: a named, distinguishable attribute.
  • L_q — the line of distinction of q: the closed interval [0, 1] together with the definitions of its two endpoints.
  • p — a position on L_q, with p ∈ [0, 1].
  • s = 2p − 1 — the signed position, with s ∈ [−1, +1].
  • k — resolution: the number of distinguishable steps on a line at a given observation.
  • ρ — the reference: the observer, the conditions and the provenance of a value.
  • n — the layer index, n = 0, 1, 2, …
  • ⊥ — unknown. Never equal to 0.

Three conventions apply throughout:

  1. A value has no meaning apart from its endpoint definitions.
  2. An unknown is recorded as ⊥, never as 0. Zero asserts isn't; ⊥ asserts nothing.
  3. Numerical equality across different qualities implies no equivalence of meaning.

2. The Limiting Conditions

Definition 2.1 (Zero). Zero, M₀, is the homogeneous reference condition: a domain in which no point, region or orientation is privileged under its symmetry group, which acts transitively on it. Zero is not absence. It is undifferentiated potential, the condition before any distinction is made.

Definition 2.2 (Mono-Maximum). Let C₀ be an initial configuration space and T the permitted transformations: distinction, polarity exchange and re-entry. Mono-Maximum is

M = Closure(C₀, T),

the smallest space that contains C₀ and is closed under T. It is maximal relative to its own rules.

Principle 2.3 (Local reproduction of the limits). Every line of distinction reproduces the global limits locally. Position 0 on L_q is the Zero of quality q, and position 1 is its Mono-Maximum. The same structure therefore recurs at every scale. This is the basis of scale independence (§7).

3. The Proto-Atomos

Definition 3.1 (Line of distinction). For a quality q, the line of distinction L_q is the interval [0, 1] with two named endpoints:

  • 0 is the infinitesimal expression of q (isn't);
  • 1 is the maximal expression of q (is).

Both endpoints must be stated in words before any position is assigned.

Definition 3.2 (Proto-atomos). A proto-atomos is the ordered quadruple

π = ⟨q, L_q, p, ρ⟩

that is, a quality, its line of distinction, a position on that line, and the reference under which the position was established.

Definition 3.3 (Self-reference). A proto-atomos refers to itself. Applying again the distinction that defines it returns the same distinction:

Δ = Δ.

This is what makes it a unit rather than an event: returning to it gives it again.

Axioms of the unit

  • PA1 (Identity). Every proto-atomos satisfies Δ = Δ under its own reference ρ.
  • PA2 (Bipolarity). Every line of distinction has exactly two limits, isn't and is. A quality that needs more reference conditions is represented by several proto-atomos, not by one line with extra poles.
  • PA3 (Boundedness). Every position satisfies 0 ≤ p ≤ 1. Unbounded intensities are brought into the interval by the standard in §4.
  • PA4 (Discreteness). Every observation has finite resolution k, so observed positions lie on the lattice {0, 1/k, 2/k, …, 1}. A proto-atomos is a discrete unit even when the quality it describes is continuous.
  • PA5 (Relationality). No proto-atomos exists without a reference ρ. Every distinction is made from somewhere.
  • PA6 (Scale independence). The definition and axioms of the unit are the same at every layer n.

4. Measurement Standard

S1 (Endpoint specification). State in words what p = 0 and p = 1 mean, and how a position is obtained.

S2 (Compactification of unbounded quantities). For an intensity x ∈ [0, ∞), use

p = φ(x) = x / (x + x₀),

where x₀ > 0 is the declared reference scale at which p = 0.5. Under this map φ(0) = 0, p approaches 1 as x grows without bound, and the map is strictly increasing and invertible, with x = x₀ · p / (1 − p). The maximal limit 1 is therefore the ideal image of infinite magnitude: approached, but never reached by any finite value.

S3 (Resolution). State k. Report positions as multiples of 1/k. Two positions closer than 1/k cannot be told apart at that observation.

S4 (Provenance). Record in ρ who or what observed, when, under what conditions, and by what method.

S5 (Status). Mark each value as observed, measured, inferred, proposed, or unknown (⊥).

S6 (Polarity is not probability). p = 0.8 is a position between two limits. It is not an 80% likelihood.

5. Composition: Compound Qualities and Layers

5.1 Juxtaposition: the descriptor vector

Definition 5.1. At layer n, a subject is described by the set of its proto-atomos:

D(subject) = { π₁, π₂, …, π_m }.

Written as a vector of positions, P = (p₁, …, p_m), each component keeps its own line definition. Juxtaposition places qualities side by side. It does not combine them.

5.2 The compound proto-atomos

Definition 5.2. A compound proto-atomos is one whose quality is defined from other proto-atomos:

π_c = ⟨c, L_c, f(p₁, …, p_m), ρ⟩,

where f : [0, 1]^m → [0, 1] is a declared aggregation rule satisfying two conditions:

  • F1 (Boundary agreement): f(0, …, 0) = 0 and f(1, …, 1) = 1.
  • F2 (Monotonicity): raising any input never lowers the output.

One of the following standard rules must be named whenever a compound is formed:

  • Weighted mean, f = Σ wᵢ pᵢ with Σ wᵢ = 1, when the qualities can substitute for one another.
  • Geometric mean, f = Π pᵢ^wᵢ, when every component is required, so that any input at 0 forces the compound to 0.
  • Minimum, f = min(pᵢ), for strict conjunction.

Proposition 5.3 (Closure of compounds). A compound proto-atomos satisfies PA1–PA6.

Proof. By F1 its output lies in [0, 1] and its limits agree with those of its inputs, so PA2 and PA3 hold. Its reference is the union of its inputs' references, so PA5 holds. Under any of the standard rules its resolution is bounded by that of its inputs, so PA4 holds. The definition does not depend on n, so PA6 holds. ∎

A compound is therefore itself a proto-atomos. This is what allows layering.

5.3 Layers

Definition 5.4. Layer 0 consists of proto-atomos measured directly. Layer n + 1 consists of proto-atomos composed from layer n, either by aggregation (§5.2) or by lifting (§7). A complete description is the whole stack of layers, down to the units measured directly.

6. Deviation and the Triangle

Proposition 6.1 (Minimality of three). A single line of distinction allows motion only toward one of its two limits. A third point off the line, a deviation, closes the smallest figure with an interior: the triangle. Three is therefore the minimum number of points for any geometric structure. The triangle is also the only polygon that cannot change its shape without changing the lengths of its sides.

Definition 6.2 (Deviation decomposition). Let an existing quality axis be the unit vector u, and let a new quality have unit direction v at angle θ to u. The new quality splits into two components:

polarity component = cos θ = ⟨u, v⟩,
deviation component = sin θ = ‖v − ⟨u, v⟩u‖.

Definition 6.3 (Residual deviation). For an existing set of orthonormal axes {u₁, …, u_r}, the residual of a new direction v is

r(v) = v − Σ ⟨uᵢ, v⟩ uᵢ

(the Gram–Schmidt step).

  • If ‖r(v)‖ ≥ ε, the quality opens a new axis, u_{r+1} = r(v) / ‖r(v)‖, and with it a new triangular cell.
  • If ‖r(v)‖ < ε, the quality is redundant at tolerance ε.

Definition 6.4 (Descriptive completeness). A subject is completely described at tolerance ε when no further candidate quality has residual deviation of at least ε. This is the stopping rule for description.

Principle 6.5 (Qualities are relational). A quality is located in a triangle, not at a vertex (see Example 8.2).

7. Lifting and Scale Independence

Definition 7.1 (Closed configuration). A set of proto-atomos and their relations is closed when it is complete at tolerance ε (Definition 6.4) and remains stable when observed again (PA1).

Definition 7.2 (Lifting). The lifting map Λ sends a closed configuration K at layer n to a single proto-atomos at layer n + 1:

Λ(K) = ⟨q_K, L_K, p_K, ρ_K⟩.

Here q_K names the configuration as one quality, L_K defines its limits, and p_K is given by a declared aggregation rule over K.

Proposition 7.3 (Scale independence). For every n, the units at layer n satisfy the same axioms as units at layer 0.

Proof. Lifting produces a proto-atomos in the sense of Definition 3.2, and Proposition 5.3 applies to its aggregation. ∎

One primitive therefore serves for a quality, an object, a concept, a system or a world. Only the layer index changes.

8. Worked Examples

8.1 A descriptor vector (layer 0)

Subject: a dog. Reference ρ: one observer, daylight, visual inspection. Resolution: k = 100.

  • Fur (0 := hairless; 1 := maximally dense coat): p = 0.90.
  • Brown (0 := no brown hue; 1 := fully brown): p = 0.67.
  • Red (0 := no red hue; 1 := fully red): p = 0.54.

The signed form is s = (0.80, 0.34, 0.08). Brown and red are separate lines. Their values are independent rather than complementary, and both can be high at once.

8.2 A relational quality: wetness

Wetness is fixed by three interfacial tensions: solid–vapor, solid–liquid and liquid–vapor. Young's relation ties them together through the contact angle θ:

γ_SV = γ_SL + γ_LV cos θ.

Wetness is therefore a triangle by construction. Define its line of distinction L_wet with 0 := complete non-wetting (θ = 180°) and 1 := complete spreading (θ = 0°), so that

p = (1 + cos θ) / 2.
  • Mercury on glass, θ ≈ 140°: p ≈ (1 − 0.766) / 2 ≈ 0.12.
  • Mercury on clean gold, which it wets and amalgamates with, θ → 0°: p → 1.
  • Water on clean glass, θ ≈ 0–20°: p ≈ 0.97–1.00.

The liquid is the same and the wetness is opposite. The quality belongs to the configuration of liquid, surface and medium, not to the liquid alone. On gold, mercury is wet in exactly the sense that water is wet on glass: both occupy the same region of L_wet.

8.3 Deviation

Let the existing axis be u = (1, 0) and let a new quality v lie at θ = 60°. Its polarity component is cos 60° = 0.500 and its deviation component is sin 60° ≈ 0.866. With ε = 0.1, the residual ‖r(v)‖ ≈ 0.866 exceeds ε, so v opens a new axis and a new triangular cell.

At θ = 3°, by contrast, the deviation is sin 3° ≈ 0.052, which is below ε. That quality is redundant at this tolerance.

8.4 A compound and a lift

At layer 0, take three proto-atomos of the dog: fur (p = 0.90), self-motion (p = 0.95) and body warmth (p = 0.85). Define the compound animate-mammalian by the geometric mean with equal weights:

p = (0.90 × 0.95 × 0.85)^(1/3) ≈ 0.90.

If this configuration is closed at tolerance ε, Λ lifts it to a single layer-1 proto-atomos, mammal, with p ≈ 0.90. That unit can now be juxtaposed with other layer-1 units, such as domesticated or quadruped.

A stuffed toy dog has self-motion p ≈ 0. Under the geometric mean the compound then falls to 0, which correctly separates the toy from the animal.

# PART II — THE UNIFIED SYSTEM

9. Genesis: From Zero to Distinction

The framework forms one generative sequence:

M₀ → Δ → π → R → D → O → … → M

That is: homogeneous Zero; the first distinction; the proto-atomos; relational organization; differentiated structure; observable organization; and, in the limit of closure, Mono-Maximum.

The step M₀ → Δ is a collapse between the two limits. The undifferentiated condition and the condition of total attainment meet in a single distinction: this, and not that. Every proto-atomos is a local instance of that collapse, a line from its own Zero to its own Mono-Maximum with a position on it.

The sequence is recursive. Any differentiated region can serve as a new homogeneous reference for further distinction:

M₀ → Δ₁ → M₁ → Δ₂ → M₂ → …

10. Correspondence with χ-RCC

The χ-Reentrant Closure Calculus is one formal lens on the system. Its proven results support the structure of the proto-atomos. Its conjectures remain conjectures here, and none of its results establishes the philosophical claims.

The proto-atomos is J. The proposed operator J (Interject) is a differentiation whose daughter returns to its source:

s → s′ → s.

This is Δ = Δ in operational form: a distinction made, and returned from, in a single act. J is the constructive form of Axiom A2 (recurrence), since it creates a recurrent pair in one step. In the reference implementation, every change the kernel admits is a paired transition of exactly this kind. Every admitted change in the calculus is therefore a proto-atomos.

Correspondences.

  • PA1, Δ = Δ: A2, made constructive by J.
  • A stable proto-atomos: a closed recurrent class C, which satisfies D(C) = C (Theorem 3).
  • The two limits of a line of distinction: two fibers over one base in a discrete fibered graph. In the reference configuration these are F_MATTER and F_ANTIMATTER. The structure is discrete, not a differential-geometric bundle.
  • Establishing the two limits: D (Differentiate).
  • The relation that closes a triangle: C (Couple), which induces mutual reachability.
  • Lifting Λ: A (Absorb), irreversible merger into a higher-order unit.
  • A retained but inactive unit (⊥): S (Suspend). Suspended elements remain on record.
  • Admissibility of operations on a unit: the χ-field, its differentiation quota, and the forbidden compositions (S, A) and (S, C).
  • Pendulation: directional flux. Coherence κ = Σ|N_f − N_r| ⁄ Σ(N_f + N_r) measures net directed motion along a line; balance β = 1 − κ measures how far that motion returns.
  • Polarity between two trajectories: directional polarity, (same − opposite) ⁄ (same + opposite), in [−1, +1].
  • The interior observer: A1 (closure) together with Theorem 3 (observation as fixed point).
  • Replayable history (§14.3): the hash-chained record of kernel decisions.
  • Scale independence (Proposition 7.3): consistent with χ-RCC Conjecture 1 (self-similarity), which is still open. Pure quotienting stabilizes after one level, so higher layers depend on operator activity, especially C.

Witness. The implementation replays 300 legacy scenarios identically, blocks unauthorized changes, detects tampering, and verifies Theorems 1, 1b and 3 on 2,459 recurrent random digraphs with no counterexamples. This shows that the operations are consistent and executable. It does not test the philosophical claims.

11. Change: Pendulation and Ripple

Definition 11.1 (Pendulation). The time-indexed position p(t) of a proto-atomos is its pendulation. It need not be periodic. In the extended form Q = (M, P), magnitude and polarity can change independently, which gives four basic cases: both stable, magnitude changing, polarity changing, or both changing.

Definition 11.2 (Trajectory). A sequence of descriptor states D(t₀) → D(t₁) → … → D(t_n) is a trajectory through the space of configurations.

Hypothesis 11.3 (Ripple). A pendulating proto-atomos is the peak or trough of a ripple: the expression, in observable reality, of patterns of higher dimension. Any subject is then a superposition of such ripples, a wave packet of qualities. This hypothesis is stated separately from the representational system (see §15).

12. The Observer

Postulate 12.1 (Interior observer). The observer lies inside every configuration it describes, and is one vertex of every triangle it constructs. In χ-RCC this follows from Axiom A1 (closure): no observation escapes the domain.

Definition 12.2 (Delta relation). Following the Delta framework, every distinction is a relation among four components: observer, reference point, conditions and trajectory. These must be kept distinguishable from one another.

Definition 12.3 (Observational displacement). For two observers a and b describing the same subject, the displacement on quality q is

δ_q = p_q(a) − p_q(b).

A non-zero displacement is data. It must be attributed to a change in the subject, a difference in reference, a difference in resolution, or a difference in timing. It must not be averaged away.

Principle 12.4. Observation establishes distinguishability. It does not create physical reality.

13. Comparison and Cross-Domain Mapping

Definition 13.1 (Distance between descriptions). Let A and B be described on aligned qualities, meaning qualities with identical line definitions, with weights w. Then

d(A, B) = √( Σ wᵢ (p_A,ᵢ − p_B,ᵢ)² ) + λ · |R_A Δ R_B|,

where the second term counts the relations present in one description but absent from the other. Qualities that appear on only one side are reported separately, as a vocabulary difference.

Definition 13.2 (Cross-domain map). A map φ from the lines of domain A to the lines of domain B is structure-preserving when it does three things:

  1. it sends limits to limits (0 to 0 and 1 to 1, or consistently reversed);
  2. it carries relations to relations;
  3. it carries pendulation to pendulation.

Standard 13.3. Every comparison reports what matches, what fails to match, and what cannot be compared. It must also outperform a random mapping on the same data before any correspondence is asserted.

Example 8.2 is a structure-preserving map. L_wet carries water on glass and mercury on gold to the same region, with the limits preserved.

14. Application: A Self-Establishing Descriptor Network

Definition 14.1 (Node). A node N is a set of proto-atomos D(N) whose first member is its own self-reference, π_self, with Δ = Δ.

Operations.

  1. Grounding. Every node begins with π_self.
  2. Self-description. The node assigns positions to its own qualities under its own reference.
  3. Relation. When nodes N₁ and N₂ register each other, the relation between them becomes a proto-atomos, held in a triangle with the observing node as one vertex.
  4. Learning by deviation. An observation adds structure only if its residual deviation is at least ε (Definition 6.3).
  5. Abstraction by lifting. Closed configurations are lifted to layer n + 1 (Definition 7.2).
  6. Distributed reference. No node holds a description of the whole. The network is established through the referenced partial descriptions of all its nodes.

Property 14.2 (Legibility). Every internal state of the network is a set of named qualities with defined limits and positions. Its reasoning can therefore be read as description, not as opaque parameters.

Property 14.3 (Replay). If every operation is recorded, every state can be reconstructed by replaying the record from π_self. In the χ-RCC implementation, that record is a hash chain of paired transitions.

15. Standards of Validity and Scope

Five forms of evaluation are kept distinct:

  1. Formal validity — the construction satisfies its definitions and axioms.
  2. Representational validity — the construction faithfully describes its subject.
  3. Observational support — the positions agree with observation within resolution.
  4. Inferential support — the conclusions follow from the definitions and premises.
  5. Empirical confirmation — independent evidence supports a physical interpretation.

The representational system (Part I and §§12–14) does not depend on Hypothesis 11.3. That polarity is a ripple of higher-dimensional pattern in spacetime is a separate physical hypothesis, and it needs independent evidence.

16. Open Interfaces and Criteria for Refutation

This conjecture is meant to be completed by many contributions, both mathematical and philosophical, not closed by a single one. Infinity enters it as a horizon that it points toward, not as a quantity it computes.

The conjecture would be weakened or refuted by any of the following:

  1. A class of distinguishable subjects that cannot be described to any tolerance ε by finite stacks of proto-atomos, or that is described no better than by a simpler baseline.
  2. Structure-preserving cross-domain maps whose degree of preservation fails to predict which transferred inferences are correct.
  3. A descriptor network with lifting that describes new subjects no more efficiently over time than one without lifting.
  4. A structure that requires a primitive which cannot be decomposed into lines of distinction and deviation.

Open interfaces invite further work on:

  • a minimal local update rule for a triangulated lattice of proto-atomos;
  • a canonical choice of aggregation rule for each class of quality;
  • whether iterated lifting reaches a fixed point (χ-ergodic) or continues indefinitely (χ-creative).

17. Condensed Identity

  • Limits: M₀ (Zero); M = Closure(C₀, T) (Mono-Maximum).
  • Primitive: π = ⟨q, L_q, p, ρ⟩, with Δ = Δ and p ∈ [0, 1].
  • Signed position: s = 2p − 1.
  • Compactification: p = x / (x + x₀).
  • Resolution: p ∈ {0, 1/k, …, 1}.
  • Compound: p_c = f(p₁, …, p_m), where f is monotone and preserves the boundaries.
  • Deviation: cos θ = ⟨u, v⟩; residual r(v) = v − Σ ⟨uᵢ, v⟩ uᵢ.
  • Completeness: ‖r(v)‖ < ε for every remaining candidate quality.
  • Lift: Λ(K) = π at layer n + 1.
  • Scale independence: PA1–PA6 hold for every n.
  • Operational form: J, s → s′ → s.

18. Conclusion

Between Zero and Mono-Maximum lies the line of distinction, and on it the proto-atomos: one quality, two limits, one position, one reference. It is discrete in observation, bounded in value, and identical in form at every scale. It forms layers by compounding, triangles by deviation, and higher-order units by lifting, and the observer is always one of its vertices.

The conjecture is that this single primitive is sufficient to relate all describable things in one geometry of quality, and that an intelligence can establish itself from its descriptors.

Status: proposed conjecture, open to formalization, implementation, refutation and extension by related conjectures.
