The Mathematical Symbol Encyclopedia

A catalogue of mathematical and physical notation: name, pronunciation, meaning and example, organised by field.

Read this first. Notation is conventional, not universal. The same glyph can mean different things in different fields (see “Context-dependent symbols”). Always check how an author defines a symbol before relying on its usual meaning.
Status. Working draft: not yet independently reviewed. Historical dates and constant values are approximate. The first section gives definition-grade entries with LaTeX and Unicode codes.
Closing note. Symbols are compressed definitions, and the same glyph can carry different meanings in different fields. Check an author’s definitions before relying on convention. Entries are grouped by field; use the search box to find a symbol, name or meaning.
